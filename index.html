The experience

Exploring the hidden hydraulic landscape

Hidden Waters is an immersive digital reconstruction of the hydraulic system associated with the Convent of Capuchos de Alferrara. The project combines historical research, architectural reconstruction and an interactive experience developed in Unreal Engine.
