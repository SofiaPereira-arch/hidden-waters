Hidden Waters

Process

From historical investigation to digital reconstruction and interactive development, the project was structured through three complementary phases.

Phase 01

Historical Research

Collection and interpretation of historical sources, architectural records, interviews and comparable hydraulic systems. This phase established the basis for reconstructing elements that are no longer accessible or fully documented.

Phase 02

Digital Reconstruction

Architectural modelling, terrain integration and reconstruction of the hydraulic route using the evidence gathered during the research phase. Revit, image documentation and comparative analysis informed the geometry transferred into Unreal Engine.

Phase 03

Interactive Experience

Development of the first-person experience, archive, interaction systems, photography, weather, time, navigation and visual atmosphere. This final phase brought the research and reconstruction together in an explorable environment.
